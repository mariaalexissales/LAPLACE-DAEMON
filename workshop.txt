title=LAPLACE//DAEMON
description=[h1]LAPLACE//DAEMON[/h1]
description=[h2]Forged blades with rarity, and a tarot spread to read them by[/h2]
description=[b]Every blade you forge rolls a rarity off your Blacksmith level. Then you give it a fortune: three tarot cards in Past, Present and Future, and where a card sits decides what it does.[/b]
description=
description=Ever put all those hours into Blacksmithing just to pull the exact same sword as everyone else? Not anymore. Two Rare swords won't even hit the same.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]
description=[b]1. Forge a blade.[/b] Any of the blacksmith blade recipes. It comes off the anvil with its rarity in the name - [i]Sword Blade - Rare (Blunt)[/i]. The higher your Blacksmith, the better your odds. Level 0 is nothing but Common and Uncommon, level 10 can land a Legendary.
description=
description=[b]2. Put it on a handle.[/b] The rarity comes along with it - [i]Sword - Rare[/i]. Take it back off and it's still the same blade with the same roll, so no, you can't reroll it by pulling it apart :P
description=
description=[b]3. Feel the roll.[/b] Every rarity comes with a damage range, as a percent of what the vanilla weapon hits for:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Common[/b] - 90 to 110%
description=[*] [b]Uncommon[/b] - 110 to 125%
description=[*] [b]Rare[/b] - 125 to 145%
description=[*] [b]Epic[/b] - 145 to 170%
description=[*] [b]Legendary[/b] - 170 to 200%
description=[/list]
description=It scales both ends of the swing, so a Legendary knife is still a knife. It's just a very, very good one.
description=
description=[b]4. Find a tarot deck.[/b] The vanilla deck, now in more places - blacksmith shops, occult and new age shelves, antique shops, carnival prizes, and the odd dresser. Now and then a zombie is carrying one too.
description=
description=[b]5. Pull a card.[/b] Right-click the deck and hit [b]Draw a Card[/b]. You pull from the top of the deck, and the rest of it goes [i]poof[/i]... as if the universe took it back.
description=
description=[b]6. Lay out the spread.[/b] Right-click your forged weapon and pick [b]Tarot Spread[/b]. Three slots: [b]Past[/b], [b]Present[/b] and [b]Future[/b]. Right-click a slot and you get every card you're carrying, with exactly what it would do [i]in that slot[/i] - real numbers, before and after, before you commit to anything.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]The Cards[/h2]
description=A card isn't three different cards. It's one card read three ways, and the slot decides how it shows up. Take [b]The Emperor[/b]:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Past - Accumulated power.[/b] Min Damage ↑, Max Damage ↑, Max Condition ↑. The weapon got strong off everything that came before.
description=[*] [b]Present - Force.[/b] Max Damage ↑↑, Knockback ↑, Attack Speed ↓. Everything goes into this swing.
description=[*] [b]Future - Impending dominance.[/b] Max Damage ↑, Knockback ↑, Crit Multiplier ↑. The next hit is the one that counts.
description=[/list]
description=Four cards are in play for now:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]The Emperor[/b] - power. Damage and knockback.
description=[*] [b]The Chariot[/b] - momentum. Attack speed and reach.
description=[*] [b]Death[/b] - the killing blow. Crit chance and crit damage.
description=[*] [b]Temperance[/b] - the sustainable weapon. Lighter, quicker, and slow to wear out.
description=[/list]
description=The other 18 Major Arcana are already drawn, just not dealt yet ^^
description=
description=[b]A few rules:[/b] a card can only sit on a weapon once, so no stacking three Deaths. Swapping a card out hands the old one back, so experiment all you want - nothing's lost.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What Counts As A Blade[/h2]
description=Long blades and short blades, the way the game sorts them - swords, short swords, machetes, knives, and the crude and scrap ones too. The meat cleaver and hand scythe are axes as far as the game is concerned, so those stay vanilla. Spears aren't in yet either.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Heads Up[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Multiplayer is new.[/b] The server rolls every rarity and owns every card, so nobody can hand themselves a Legendary - but it has had far less time in the wild than singleplayer. Try it on a server you don't mind breaking first, and tell me what you find.
description=[*] [b]Two mods in one.[/b] Enable both [b]LAPLACE//DAEMON Core[/b] and [b]LAPLACE//DAEMON Arcana[/b]. Core runs on its own if you only want the rarities, Arcana needs Core.
description=[*] [b]The numbers are a first pass.[/b] Rarity odds, damage ranges, how hard each arrow hits. If something feels off, tell me!
description=[/list]
