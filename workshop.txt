description=[h1]LAPLACE//DAEMON[/h1]
description=[h2]Your forge is a loot drop now. Every blade rolls a rarity, and a roll of its own.[/h2]
description=[b]Every blade you forge rolls Common to Legendary off your Blacksmith level, then rolls its own damage inside that tier. Then you read it its fortune: three tarot cards in Past, Present and Future.[/b]
description=
description=Ever put all those hours into Blacksmithing just to pull the exact same sword as everyone else? Not anymore. Two Rare swords won't even hit the same.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Rarity: Common to Legendary[/h2]
description=Every blade comes off the anvil with its tier stamped right in the name - [i]Sword Blade - Legendary (Blunt)[/i]. Put it on a handle and it's a [i]Sword - Legendary[/i]. Five tiers, and each one is a damage band, as a percent of what the vanilla weapon hits for:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Common[/b] - 90 to 110%
description=[*] [b]Uncommon[/b] - 110 to 125%
description=[*] [b]Rare[/b] - 125 to 145%
description=[*] [b]Epic[/b] - 145 to 170%
description=[*] [b]Legendary[/b] - 170 to 200%
description=[/list]
description=It scales both ends of the swing, so a Legendary knife is still a knife. It's just a very, very good one.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]The Roll: No Two Blades Alike[/h2]
description=Rarity is the tier. The roll is what you actually got. Inside its band every blade rolls its own number, down to the percent - a Legendary can land anywhere from 170 to 200, 31 different ways. So there's the Legendary you forged... and then there's the [i]god roll[/i] Legendary you're still chasing :P
description=
description=And it's locked the second it leaves the anvil. Put it on a handle, take it back off - same blade, same roll. No, you can't reroll it by pulling it apart.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Your Blacksmith Level Is Your Loot Table[/h2]
description=Level 0 is nothing but Common and Uncommon. Every level after that pushes the odds up the ladder:
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Blacksmith[/th][th]Common[/th][th]Uncommon[/th][th]Rare[/th][th]Epic[/th][th]Legendary[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td]0[/td][td]95%[/td][td]5%[/td][td]-[/td][td]-[/td][td]-[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]4[/td][td]60%[/td][td]30%[/td][td]9%[/td][td]1%[/td][td]-[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]6[/td][td]40%[/td][td]33%[/td][td]20%[/td][td]6%[/td][td]1%[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td]10[/td][td]10%[/td][td]22%[/td][td]35%[/td][td]24%[/td][td]9%[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=Rare opens up at 2, Epic at 4, Legendary at 6. By level 10, one blade in three comes out Epic or better. Go grind that anvil~
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]What Rolls[/h2]
description=Anything you forge into a melee weapon - blades, spears, axes, maces, hammers, even the meat cleaver. It's the head that rolls, handle or no handle.
description=
description=[b]Forged armor rolls too[/b], cuirass to codpiece: the roll scales bite, scratch and bullet defense (never past 100) and max condition.
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=[h2]Then Read Its Fortune[/h2]
description=The roll decides how hard it hits. The cards decide how it fights.
description=
description=[b]1. Find a tarot deck.[/b] The vanilla deck, now in more places - blacksmith shops, occult shelves, antique shops, carnival prizes, the odd dresser. Now and then a zombie is carrying one too.
description=
description=[b]2. Pull a card.[/b] Right-click the deck and hit [b]Draw a Card[/b]. One card off the top, and the rest of the deck goes [i]poof[/i]... as if the universe took it back. Which card you get is the universe's call too.
description=
description=[b]3. Lay out the spread.[/b] Right-click your forged weapon or armor and pick [b]Tarot Spread[/b]. Three slots: [b]Past[/b], [b]Present[/b] and [b]Future[/b]. Right-click a slot and you get every card you're carrying, with exactly what it would do [i]in that slot[/i] - real numbers, before and after, before you commit to anything.
description=
description=[h3]One Card, Three Readings[/h3]
description=A card isn't three different cards. It's one card read three ways, and the slot decides how it shows up. Take [b]The Emperor[/b]:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Past - Accumulated power.[/b] Min Damage ↑, Max Damage ↑, Max Condition ↑. The weapon got strong off everything that came before.
description=[*] [b]Present - Force.[/b] Max Damage ↑↑, Knockback ↑, Attack Speed ↓. Everything goes into this swing.
description=[*] [b]Future - Impending dominance.[/b] Max Damage ↑, Knockback ↑, Crit Multiplier ↑. The next hit is the one that counts.
description=[/list]
description=Six cards are in play for now:
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]The Emperor[/b] - power. Damage and knockback, or defense on armor.
description=[*] [b]The Chariot[/b] - momentum. Attack speed and reach.
description=[*] [b]Death[/b] - the killing blow. Crit chance and crit damage.
description=[*] [b]Temperance[/b] - the sustainable one. Lighter and quicker, weapon or armor.
description=[*] [b]The Empress[/b] - protection. Warmth and defense, armor only.
description=[*] [b]The Hierophant[/b] - discipline. Defense all round, armor only.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Better blade, better reading.[/b] A card's damage arrows are a share of the blade [i]after[/i] its roll, so the same Emperor hits harder on a Legendary. The forge and the fortune stack.
description=
description=[b]A few rules:[/b] a card can only sit on a weapon once, so no stacking three Deaths. Swapping a card out hands the old one back, so experiment all you want - nothing's lost.
description=
description=The other 16 Major Arcana are already drawn, just not dealt yet ^^
description=
